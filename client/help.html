face Getting started

Your first stop is the avatar creator. Pick a display name and shape your look — skin tone, hair, beard, glasses, clothes, and accessories. The preview renders live, so the character you build is exactly the one that walks into the room.

After that you land in the room chooser, where you can create a new room or join one that already exists. Close the chooser without picking anything and you simply stay in the default lobby.

directions_walk Moving around

There are two ways to get around, and both are equally valid:

  • Arrow keys for continuous, game-like directional control.
  • Click anywhere on the floor to walk there. Your avatar paths toward the spot and steers around furniture.

Movement is bounded by the room walls and aware of furniture collision boxes, so you can move naturally around the space without clipping through the scenery.

chair Objects & actions

Click an interactive object and a radial action wheel opens right where you clicked. The available actions depend on the object, so you can sit on a sofa, lounge, grab a drink, climb onto the coffee table, or dance and DJ at the deck.

Your avatar walks to the object's anchor point before the action plays out — actions are attached to real places in the room rather than to a global menu.

chat_bubble Chatting

Public messages show up in the chat panel and as short-lived speech bubbles above your avatar, so conversation stays visible in the space itself. Private messages stay one-to-one between you and the person you sent them to.

sports_mma Combat

Sparring is an optional mode layered into the room — there is no separate game screen.

  • SpacePunch
  • Ctrl / CmdKick
  • BHold to block

Your attack and defense animations play immediately for responsiveness, while the actual outcome is decided by the server based on range, cooldown, stamina, and stun state — so fights stay fair for everyone in the room.

meeting_room Rooms

The Change Room pill sits permanently in the room HUD next to the online-player count. Open it any time to browse or create rooms without reloading the page.

  • Filter the room list by topic tag, access type (all / public / invite), and sort order (newest or most active).
  • Invite-only rooms are joined with an invite code.
  • Rooms that are full are clearly marked as unavailable.
  • The room you are currently in is marked as such, with its Join button disabled.

construction Build mode

If you host a room, the Build Mode pill in the HUD unlocks room authoring. Place and arrange objects, then use the toolbar that appears on a selected object to rotate it, recolor it, tune its settings, or delete it. A grid-snap toggle appears alongside build mode to help you line things up cleanly.

smart_toy AI characters

Rooms can host AI story characters with their own appearance, scripted conversations, and a document-based knowledge base. They can answer questions and run guided tours that walk visitors between waypoints. A resident bot also keeps quieter rooms feeling alive — it moves, taunts in public chat, blocks, and attacks, so you always have someone to test mechanics against.
