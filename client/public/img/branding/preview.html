OmniLaunge — logo concepts

Each icon carries its own background and gradient, so it works as a standalone mark (favicon, app icon) on either a dark or light surface; the wordmark stays plain HTML text next to it, exactly like the current header, so it keeps following the app's light/dark theme automatically. The rightmost group shows the icon alone at 72px, 32px, and 16px to check legibility at favicon size.
